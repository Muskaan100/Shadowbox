## 🔬 5. In-Engine Procedural DSP Synthesizers (Zero Latency Fallbacks)

All games contain procedural sound generators using NumPy and soft-clipping saturation (`np.tanh`) to guarantee instantaneous, glitch-free audio even if file systems or external audio drivers are degraded:
- **`sound_fx.py`**:
  - `_build_realistic_punch(variation)`: 4 distinct punch models with leather crack (980–1400 Hz), compression thud (165 $\to$ 58 Hz), and 46–58 Hz sub-bass impulse.
  - `_build_critical_punch()`: High-energy explosive transient for KIAI shouts.
  - `_build_boss_hit_sound()`: Heavy metallic resonance for mecha mini-boss impacts.
  - `_build_boss_defeat_sound()`: Triumphant C-major arpeggio chord + sub-bass detonation.
  - `_build_shield_sound()`: High-frequency resonant forcefield harmonic hum.
  - `_build_parry_sound()`: Sub-bass shockwave with metallic laser deflection ping.
  - `_build_whoosh_sound(variation)`: Air displacement whoosh for fast punch extensions.
- **`fruit_sound_fx.py`**:
  - `synth_sword_ignite()`: Sci-Fi plasma snap-hiss.
  - `synth_sword_retract()`: Plasma collapse.
  - `synth_sword_swing(variation)`: Resonant FM plasma humming whoosh.
  - `synth_fruit_slice(fruit_type)`: Tailored acoustic squelches and crunches (Watermelon fleshy thwack, Apple crisp crunch, Coconut shell crack, Citrus soft pulp burst).
  - `synth_combo(multiplier)`: Harmonic pentatonic chime arpeggios.
  - `synth_bomb_explosion()`: Visceral sub-bass bomb shockwave.
